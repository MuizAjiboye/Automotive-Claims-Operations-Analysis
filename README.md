# 🚗 Automotive Claims Performance Analysis

## Executive Summary

A leading automotive operations team required greater visibility into claims activity, financial exposure, and data quality issues impacting operational efficiency.

An analysis was conducted on over **319,000 claims representing €14 million in claim value** to evaluate claims performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and improve decision-making across internal and external processing teams.

The review focused on five key business areas: claims volume, claims value, operational ownership, country-level performance, and data quality. The findings revealed opportunities to improve reporting accuracy, strengthen data governance, and optimize claims operations through automation and proactive monitoring.

---

## Business Context

The Claims Operations team was experiencing growing reporting complexity as claim volumes increased across multiple countries and business units.

Leadership required answers to several critical questions:

- Which countries generate the highest claim volumes and costs?
- How are internal and external teams performing?
- What data quality issues are creating operational risk?
- Where should operational improvements be prioritized?
- How can reporting be automated to improve decision-making?

To support these objectives, a centralized reporting solution was developed using Salesforce operational data and Power BI Semantic Models.

# Key Findings

## Internal Teams Handle The Majority Of Operational Workload

Internal operations processed approximately **69% of total claims**, while external providers accounted for the remaining **31%**.

### Why This Matters

Because most operational activity is managed internally, improvements in internal workflows have the greatest potential impact on efficiency, turnaround times, and operational costs.

### Recommendation

Prioritize process optimization initiatives within internal claims teams, including workflow standardization and automated case routing.

---

## Claims Activity Exceeded €14 Million In Total Value

Claims reviewed during the reporting period accounted for more than **€14 million** in financial exposure across over **319,000 cases**.

### Why This Matters

Even minor improvements in processing accuracy and operational efficiency can produce meaningful financial benefits.

### Recommendation

Implement proactive monitoring for high-value claim categories and investigate recurring sources of claim costs.

---

## Data Quality Issues Continue To Impact Operational Performance

Data-quality monitoring identified recurring operational challenges, including:

- Missing VIN information
- Invalid VIN formats
- Duplicate claim records
- Incomplete case submissions

### Why This Matters

Poor-quality records create downstream inefficiencies, increase manual review effort, and reduce stakeholder confidence in reporting.

### Recommendation

Introduce automated validation rules within operational workflows to prevent issues at source.

---

## Significant Differences Exist Between Markets

Country-level analysis revealed substantial differences in both claim volume and claim value across markets.

France and Germany represented the largest concentrations of operational activity, while other countries displayed significantly lower claim volumes.

### Why This Matters

Different markets create different operational and financial risks.

### Recommendation

Develop country-specific operational strategies based on claim demand, claim value, and resource requirements.

---

## Performance Trends Help Surface Emerging Operational Risks

Monitoring claim trends across ownership groups and categories revealed changes in activity patterns that may indicate operational bottlenecks or process issues.

### Why This Matters

Without trend monitoring, leadership would identify performance issues only after operational impact occurs.

### Recommendation

Establish monthly performance reviews using automated dashboards and KPI alerts.

# Technical Environment

The reporting solution was built using operational claims data sourced from **Salesforce** and modeled through **Power BI Semantic Models**.

The semantic model centralized business logic, KPI definitions, and reporting calculations, ensuring consistent reporting across operational and leadership stakeholders.

Automated reporting workflows replaced manual spreadsheet processes and provided scalable, refreshable analytics for ongoing operational monitoring.
